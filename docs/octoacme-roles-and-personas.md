# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Tech Leads guide technical design and implementation so the team can deliver reliable solutions within agreed product goals and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Break down technical work and identify design choices, dependencies, and risks
- Support Developers through implementation, reviews, and technical decisions
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability and quality practices

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Work with the PM on dependencies and delivery risks, the Product Manager on feasibility and trade-offs, Developers on implementation, QA/Testing on coverage, and Stakeholders on technical implications. Own technical decisions within agreed scope and standards; escalate changes to scope, priorities, or commitments to the PM and Product Manager.

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape usable, accessible experiences that address user needs and support product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and create user flows, wireframes, or prototypes
- Define interaction and visual design guidance, including accessibility considerations
- Gather feedback and refine designs with the delivery team

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Align with the Product Manager on user outcomes, the PM on design milestones, Developers on feasible handoffs, QA/Testing on usability and accessibility checks, and Stakeholders on feedback. Recommend design solutions; product priorities and scope remain with the Product Manager.

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts turn stakeholder needs into clear, verifiable requirements that guide delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and constraints
- Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria with the product and delivery team
- Track requirement changes and highlight gaps or dependencies

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Work with Stakeholders to validate needs, the Product Manager to align requirements with outcomes and priorities, the PM on scope and dependencies, Developers on implementation details, and QA/Testing on testable acceptance criteria. Clarify and trace requirements; prioritization and scope approval stay with the Product Manager and authorized stakeholders.

---

## Security/Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Reviewers assess proposed changes against applicable security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant controls and review designs or changes for gaps
- Document findings, required mitigations, and evidence needed for compliance
- Advise the team on risk and escalate unresolved issues

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Partner with the Tech Lead and Developers on mitigations, QA/Testing on security validation, the PM on review timing and risks, the Product Manager on product trade-offs, and Stakeholders on applicable obligations. Identify and escalate blockers; risk acceptance or exceptions require the designated authority, not the reviewer alone.

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment activities so changes can be delivered safely and communicated clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release plan, readiness checklist, and deployment coordination
- Confirm release notes, verification steps, and rollback or mitigation plans are prepared
- Coordinate release status and post-deployment follow-up

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Coordinate timing and dependencies with the PM, release scope and notes with the Product Manager, deployment and rollback plans with Developers, verification with QA/Testing, operational readiness with Support/Operations, and communications with Stakeholders. Recommend release readiness and escalate unmet gates; only designated approvers may authorize exceptions or a go/no-go decision.

---

## Support/Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support/Operations Representatives prepare teams to operate and support delivered changes after release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify support, monitoring, alerting, and runbook needs
- Review operational readiness and communicate known issues or service impacts
- Share customer and incident feedback with the project team

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Work with Developers and Tech Leads on observability and runbooks, QA/Testing on operational checks, the PM on readiness and handoffs, the Product Manager on customer impact, and Stakeholders on support expectations. Raise readiness gaps and provide operational input; product scope and release approval remain with their accountable owners.
